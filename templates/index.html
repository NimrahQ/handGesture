01 / Material

One sheet.
Printed, cut, creased.

Every frame starts as a flat printed face. The folds are already there — pressed into the paper before the camera ever sees your hand.

A — PRINT

The face

A single portrait printed edge to edge on thin stock, so light passes through the creases.

B — CREASE

The folds

Hand-scored lines that decide where the paper gives way first, and where it holds out.

C — FILM

The sequence

The crumple is filmed once, frame by frame, then scrubbed live by how closed your hand is.

02 / Response

Your fist is
the timeline.

The camera reads how closed your hand is, from 0 to 100%, and maps it straight onto the paper.

  1. Open hand
    Fingers spread. The paper lies flat and still.
  2. Begin folding
    The first creases lift at the corners.
  3. Folding
    The face bends inward along its scored lines.
  4. Almost closed
    Features disappear into the folds.
  5. Full fist
    Nothing flat is left.
03 / Build

How it works.

A small Flask server, a webcam, and hand landmark tracking — nothing else.

1

Capture

Pressing Show Hand calls /start_camera and OpenCV opens your webcam.

2

Track

Hand landmarks are detected every frame, for either hand, front or back.

3

Measure

Fingertip distances become a single closedness value, served from /hand.

4

Crumble

That value picks the frame streamed from /video_feed — the paper follows your hand.
